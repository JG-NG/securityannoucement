Published 10 November 2026

Trading is back to normal

Here’s what happened over the past week, what it means for your orders, and what we’re doing about it.

  • Gift card trades and payouts are running again
  • No impact on balances or personal data found so far
  • Over 90% of reported order issues resolved

What happened

From 3 November, our systems were targeted by an external attack. The attackers exploited PHP2Shell, a serious, newly disclosed security flaw in PHP — the software that powers a large share of the world’s websites and apps. Platforms around the world were affected, and PHP’s maintainers and cybersecurity agencies in several countries issued urgent warnings.

For a period, parts of the app became unstable and some gift card trades were settled late. We know this made selling your cards harder than it should have been, and we’re sorry.

This happened on our platform, and we take full responsibility for it.

Is my money and information safe?

As soon as the attack was detected, our technical and security teams closed the vulnerability and ran a full review of our systems.

So far, the investigation has found no impact on user balances or personal information. We’re continuing to monitor and verify, and if anything changes, we’ll let you know straight away.

Can I trade now?

Yes. The platform’s main features are working normally again, including gift card trades and settlement. You can trade as usual.

What about my delayed order?

More than 90% of the reports we received have already been resolved. Affected orders are being verified and compensated according to our plan.

For any order that’s still outstanding, we’ll check it individually and contact you directly until it’s fully sorted. Getting the system back up doesn’t mean we stop looking out for you.

Still having a problem with an order? Contact customer service in the app or send a message to our official account, and include your order number. These cases are being handled first.

What we’re doing next

This incident has reminded us that security and your trust can never be taken for granted. Going forward, we will:

  • Keep strengthening our security and patch new vulnerabilities quickly
  • Monitor trading activity for risks in real time
  • Respond faster when an order looks unusual

Thank you

Thank you for your patience, understanding and support while we worked through this. As a thank-you, we’ll soon run an appreciation event for all users — keep an eye out for the official announcement.

We’ll keep earning your trust through what we do.

The Jaragift Team
